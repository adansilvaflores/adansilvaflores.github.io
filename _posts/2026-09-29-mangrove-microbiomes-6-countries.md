Mangroves are blue carbon hotspots: they store large amounts of carbon in their sediments, and the microbes living there help decide how much of that carbon stays buried.

I wanted to know whether mangrove sediment microbiomes look the same across the world, so I reanalyzed **public shotgun metagenomes** from mangrove sediments in **Brazil, China, India, Malaysia, Mexico and Saudi Arabia**.

## What I compared

OTU abundance (log scale) across:

- Country and site
- Latitude and longitude
- Soil type (bulk soil vs. rhizosphere)
- Sediment depth
- Plant species
