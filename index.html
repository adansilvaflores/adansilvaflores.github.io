Projects

Mangrove sediment microbiomes across 6 countries

Reanalysis of public shotgun metagenomes from mangrove sediments in Brazil, China, India, Malaysia, Mexico and Saudi Arabia, comparing OTU abundance across country, site, latitude, longitude, soil type (bulk vs. rhizosphere), depth and plant species.
